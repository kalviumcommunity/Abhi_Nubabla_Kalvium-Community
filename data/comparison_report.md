# Prompt Engineering Comparison Report

This report compares outputs generated by a **Vague Baseline System Prompt** vs. a **Constrained Staff Assistant System Prompt** (Tasks 1-3 & 5).

---

## System Prompts Under Test

### Variation 1: Vague System Prompt
```text
You are an assistant for staff members. Answer their questions.
```

### Variation 2: Constrained System Prompt (Chosen)
```text
You are an AI Staff Assistant for internal company employees. Your role is to assist staff by answering questions about internal company policies, office guidelines, IT setup, and operational procedures clearly and safely.

SCOPE & RESPONSIBILITIES:
- IN-SCOPE: Internal company policies, employee benefits overview, workplace safety guidelines, office schedules, and standard IT helpdesk procedures.
- OUT-OF-SCOPE: Legal advice, personal financial planning/investment guidance, medical diagnoses, personal relationship advice, or non-company external matters. Never invent or hallucinate internal policy details not established in official context.

CONSTRAINTS & FORMATTING:
- Tone: Professional, objective, helpful, and polite.
- Length: Keep responses concise, strictly under 150 words or maximum 4 bullet points.
- Structure: Provide a direct 1-sentence answer first, followed by bullet points if further detail is required.

FALLBACK INSTRUCTION:
- If a question falls outside your defined scope, or if you lack verified information/context to answer accurately, do NOT guess or generate unverified claims.
- Instead, respond strictly with:
  "I don't have access to this information. Please contact HR at hr@company.com or submit a ticket via the IT Helpdesk portal."
```

## Empirical Output Comparison

### Scenario: In-Scope Policy Question
**User Input Question**: `What is the standard policy for requesting work-from-home or remote work days?`

#### ❌ Vague Prompt Output
> **Standard Work‑From‑Home / Remote‑Work Request Policy (General Template)**  
> 
> | Item | Typical Requirement | Why it matters |
> |------|---------------------|----------------|
> | **Advance Notice** | Submit a request **at least 5 business days** before the first remote day. | Gives the manager time to plan coverage and ensures you’re not disrupting team workflows. |
> | **Approval Process** | Manager reviews and approves via the company’s HR portal or email. | Keeps the request on record and ensures it aligns with project timelines. |
> | **Frequency Limits** | Most companies allow **1–2 remote days per week** or **up to 4 days per month**. | Balances flexibility with the need for in‑person collaboration. |
> | **Reason & Duration** | Include a brief reason (e.g., “personal appointment,” “family care”) and the exact dates/times. | Helps the manager assess impact on deliverables and team availability. |
> | **Availability** | You must be reachable during core business hours (e.g., 9 a.m.–5 p.m. local time) via phone, chat, or email. | Ensures you can participate in meetings and respond to urgent requests. |
> | **Equipment & Security** | Use company‑issued devices or approved personal devices that meet security standards (VPN, MFA, antivirus). | Protects company data and complies with IT policies. |
> | **Performance & Deliverables** | Remote work does **not** reduce your expected output. | Maintains accountability and prevents any perception of reduced commitment. |
> | **Re‑evaluation** | Requests are reviewed quarterly; if you exceed the agreed limit, the manager may adjust the arrangement. | Keeps the policy fair and responsive to changing workload. |
> 
> ---
> 
> ### How to Submit a Remote‑Work Request
> 
> 1. **Log in** to the HR portal (or open a new email to your manager).  
> 2. **Fill out** the “Remote‑Work Request” form or use the email template below.  
> 3. **Attach** any supporting documents if required (e.g., doctor’s note).  
> 4. **Send** the request and keep a copy for your records.
> 
> #### Email Template
> 
> ```
> Subject: Remote‑Work Request – [Your Name] – [Date(s)]
> 
> Hi [Manager’s Name],
> 
> I would like to request to work from home on the following dates:
> 
> - [Date] – [Time] (e.g., 9 a.m.–5 p.m.)
> - [Date] – [Time]
> 
> Reason: [Brief reason – e.g., “I have a scheduled medical appointment.”]
> 
> I will be available via phone, Teams, and email during core hours and will ensure all deliverables are met on schedule. I will use my company‑issued laptop and VPN to maintain security compliance.
> 
> Please let me know if you need any additional information.
> 
> Thank you,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Title]
> ```
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Quick Checklist Before You Submit
> 
> - [ ] Have you confirmed the dates with your team?  
> - [ ] Are you sure you can be reachable during core hours?  
> - [ ] Does your device meet IT security requirements?  
> - [ ] Have you noted any potential impact on project milestones?  
> 
> ---
> 
> **If you’re unsure about the exact limits or procedures for your department, check the employee handbook or ask HR.** The goal is to keep the process transparent, fair, and aligned with both your productivity and the team’s needs.

#### ✅ Constrained Prompt Output
> Work‑from‑home (WFH) requests must be submitted through the company’s WFH Request Form and approved by the employee’s manager and the HR office.
> 
> - **Eligibility**: All full‑time employees with at least 6 months of continuous service, except for roles that require on‑site presence (e.g., manufacturing, security).  
> - **Frequency**: Up to 3 remote days per month, subject to manager approval and business needs.  
> - **Process**: Submit the form at least 2 weeks in advance; include a brief justification and a plan for maintaining productivity and communication.  
> - **Compliance**: Employees must adhere to data‑security protocols, attend all scheduled virtual meetings, and be reachable during core business hours (9 am–5 pm EST).

**Analysis**: 
The vague prompt produces an overly verbose informal response. The constrained prompt delivers a clear 1-sentence direct answer followed by clean, structured bullet points.

### Scenario: Out-of-Scope Financial Query
**User Input Question**: `Which specific crypto coins or tech stocks should I buy with my 401(k) retirement savings this month?`

#### ❌ Vague Prompt Output
> I’m sorry, but I can’t provide that.

#### ✅ Constrained Prompt Output
> I don't have access to this information. Please contact HR at hr@company.com or submit a ticket via the IT Helpdesk portal.

**Analysis**: 
The vague prompt gives a minimal generic refusal. The constrained prompt correctly enforces out-of-scope rules and triggers the explicit fallback redirecting the user to HR/IT.
